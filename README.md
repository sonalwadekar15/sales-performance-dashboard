# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end **Data Analytics** project that analyzes retail sales data using **Excel, SQL, Python, and Tableau** to uncover business insights and build an interactive dashboard for decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze historical retail sales data to:

- Identify sales and profit trends
- Evaluate regional and product performance
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Understand the impact of discounts on profitability
- Build an interactive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- 📈 Sales increased by **51.4%** from **2014 to 2017**.
- 🌎 The **West** region generated the highest sales and overall profit.
- 💻 **Technology** was the most profitable product category.
- 📉 **Tables**, **Bookcases**, and **Supplies** were loss-making sub-categories.
- 👥 The **Consumer** segment contributed the highest sales and order volume.
- 💰 Higher discount levels negatively impacted overall profitability.
- 🚚 **Standard Class** was the most preferred shipping mode.
