Sales Performance Dashboard Documentation

1. Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore sales dataset to evaluate business performance and uncover actionable insights. The workflow involved cleaning and preparing the data, performing exploratory and SQL-based analysis, creating visualizations using Python, and building an interactive Tableau dashboard to support data-driven decision making.


2. Business Objective

The objective of this project was to:
Analyze sales and profit performance.
Identify trends across years, regions, categories, and customer segments.
Detect loss-making products and business opportunities.
Build an interactive dashboard for business users to monitor key performance indicators.

10. Key Business Insights
Sales increased by 51.4%, growing from ₹484K in 2014 to ₹733K in 2017, indicating consistent business growth.
The West region generated the highest sales and profit, while South had the lowest sales.
Technology was the most profitable category, whereas Furniture generated high sales but comparatively low profit.
Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies recorded negative profit, highlighting the need to review pricing, discounts, or costs.
The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales, profit, and order volume.
Higher discount levels were frequently associated with lower or negative profit, indicating excessive discounting impacted profitability.
Standard Class was the most preferred shipping mode, contributing the highest sales and order volume.
Improving the performance of loss-making products and underperforming regions presents an opportunity to increase overall profitability.
